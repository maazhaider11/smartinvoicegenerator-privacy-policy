# Privacy Policy for Smart Invoice Generator

  **Last updated: October 9, 2026**

  This Privacy Policy describes how **Feedo – Fee Manager** (the "App"), the
  Android
  application with package name `com.maaz.smartinvoice`, developed and published
  by
  **TechZam Solution** (the "Developer", "we", "us"), handles your information.

  By downloading or using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do
  not
  agree, please do not use the App.

  ---

  ## Summary

  Feedo – Fee Manager is a fee-management tool for tutors, coaching centres,
  gyms
  and other small institutes. **All the information you enter is stored only on
  your own device.** We do not run servers, we do not have user accounts, and we
  do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data. The App contains
  no
  advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.

  ---

  ## Information You Enter (Stored Locally on Your Device)

  To let you manage fees, the App lets you save the following on your device.
  This
  information never leaves your device except when **you** choose to share it
  (see
  "Sharing Receipts" below):

  - **Institute / business profile:** institute or business name, email address,
    phone number, logo image, and the payment details you choose to add (for
    example a UPI ID, PayPal link, other payment link, or bank details).
  - **Student / member records:** name, guardian/parent name, phone number,
    class/batch, monthly fee, join date, and any notes you add.
  - **Fee & payment records:** amounts due, amounts paid, payment method, dates,
    and generated receipt numbers.

  This data is saved in the App's local storage on your device. It is **not**
  uploaded to us or to any third party.

  ---

  ## How This Information Is Used

  The information above is used solely on your device to:

  - show your students/members and their monthly paid/unpaid status;
  - calculate fees, dues, discounts and balances;
  - generate PDF fee receipts; and
  - help you send receipts and reminders through apps you already use.

  ---

  ## Sharing Receipts and Reminders

  When you tap to share a receipt or send a reminder, the App uses your device's
  standard "share" feature (or opens a link, e.g. WhatsApp) so **you** can send
  it
  through an app you choose. The Developer is not involved in and does not
  receive
  a copy of anything you send. Any data you share this way is then handled under
  the privacy policy of the app you send it through.

  ---

  ## Device Permissions

  The App may request the following, only to provide its features:

  - **Photos / media / storage:** to let you pick a logo image and to save or
    share PDF receipts you generate.
  - **Internet:** to open links you choose (for example a WhatsApp message or a
    payment link), and, if you purchase a subscription, to verify that purchase
    (see "Subscriptions" below). The App does not transmit your student or fee
    data over the internet.

  You can manage permissions at any time in your device's Settings.

  ---

  ## Subscriptions

  The App is free to use. If an optional paid ("Pro") subscription is offered,
  any
  purchase is processed by **Google Play Billing**, and subscription status is
  managed using **RevenueCat** (RevenueCat, Inc.). Payment is handled entirely
  by
  Google Play — **we never receive or store your card or payment details.** For
  how
  these providers handle data, see Google Play's and RevenueCat's own privacy
  policies. The App shows no advertising.

  ---

  ## Data Storage, Retention and Deletion

  - Your data stays on your device for as long as the App is installed.
  - **Uninstalling the App deletes the data stored on your device.**
  - You can also delete individual students, payments, or your profile from
  within
    the App.

  Because your data is stored only on your device, you are responsible for
  keeping
  your own backups (for example by exporting or saving receipts). The Developer
  cannot recover data lost from your device.

  ---

  ## Data Security

  Your records are kept in the App's private storage on your device and are
  protected by your device's own security (such as your screen lock). No method
  of
  electronic storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
  ---

  ## Children's Privacy

  The App is a business/productivity tool intended for institute owners and
  staff.
  It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal
  information from children.

  ---

  ## Third-Party Services

  The App does not include advertising networks or analytics SDKs. The only
  third-party services that may be involved are **Google Play Billing** and
  **RevenueCat**, and only if you choose to purchase an optional subscription,
  as
  described under "Subscriptions".
